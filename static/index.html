A little guidance

From photo to relief

Upload a clear photo, wait for the local depth estimate, then adjust its size and surface. Inspect the 3D result before exporting STL or OBJ.

Move around in 3D

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and right-drag to pan. Use Fit, Reset or the top-view button to find your model again. Painting happens only in Edit view.

Correct the subject or the depth

Background tools paint what belongs to the subject. Sculpt tools raise, lower, smooth or flatten depth. Ctrl/Cmd Z undoes the selected layer; Shift adds redo. Escape closes dialogs or cancels a stroke.

Keep expectations grounded

One photo cannot reveal every contour. Presets change existing settings; they do not reconstruct missing anatomy, reflections or ornament details. A valid solid still needs inspection in your CAM software.
